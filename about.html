About Your Instructor

Welcome to Industrial and Technical Education at Isle High School.

I am a Career and Technical Education teacher with a strong interest in hands-on learning, manufacturing, engineering, technology, aviation, design, and skilled trades. My goal is to connect classroom learning to the skills and expectations students will encounter in industry, technical education, college, and everyday life.

Students learn to identify problems, plan, work safely, select tools and materials, build or repair accurately, inspect their work, troubleshoot, and improve.
