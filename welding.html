Welding / Manufacturing

Safety • Skill • Fabrication • Production • Quality

Course Overview

A hands-on block course combining welding and manufacturing. Students develop safe fabrication skills through measurement, joint preparation, welding processes, inspection, production methods, manufacturing systems, quality control, and continuous improvement.

This Week

Major Areas

  • PPE and shop safety
  • Measurement and layout
  • Joint preparation
  • SMAW and GMAW
  • Fabrication
  • Manufacturing processes
  • Production planning
  • Inspection and quality control
  • Process improvement

Course Sequence

The course begins with welding and transitions into manufacturing. The same class page and weekly calendar are used throughout the course.
